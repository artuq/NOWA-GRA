## What Went Well
- **Web-export spike delivered a real GO verdict on day 1** (10-1) — 6.8 MB brotli initial vs. the ≤10 MB gate, 3 real glyph/layout bugs found and fixed via the visual pass. Directly followed Sprint 9's Action Item #2.
- **Commit discipline held** — 33 commits across Sprint 11's window vs. Sprint 9's "zero commits all day" incident. Sprint 9's Action Item #1 clearly took.
- **The era loop's backend is real and tested, not just designed** — BurnoutSystem, PrestigeSystem's grant machinery, and ChallengeSystem all shipped with unit + integration coverage in the same sessions they were designed, following the established "wiring-AC" discipline from Sprint 8 onward.
- **9:16 portrait web presentation shipped clean** (10-2) — custom HTML shell, pillarbox background, user-verified in browser.

## What Went Poorly
- **QA and retro cadence stopped completely after Sprint 9.** No `qa-plan`, no `qa-signoff`, no `smoke-*` report, no retro exists anywhere for Sprint 10 or Sprint 11 — the most complex systems in the project (the full Burnout→Prestige→Challenge era loop) shipped with zero recorded QA sign-off. This is a bigger version of Sprint 9's own "zero commits" finding: that was one day of unprotected work; this was two sprints of unprotected process.
- **`sprint-status.yaml` went stale the same day it was written and nobody noticed for 12 days.** 11-2 and 11-4 sat marked `backlog` while fully implemented and tested code existed for both — discovered only when Sprint 12's `/gate-check` (2026-07-23) tried to reason about what was actually done.
- **11-3 and 11-5 were marked `ready-for-dev`/scheduled but never actually started**, and nothing flagged the gap until the same `/gate-check` pass. The Challenge system's backend (11-4) shipped with no way for a player to actually reach it (11-5's UI) — a fully-built system nobody could play, for 12 days.
- **Performance pass (10-3) carried a 2nd time into Sprint 11's risk register and then again into Sprint 12** — still blocked on no physical Android device, now 3 sprints running.

## Process Improvements
1. **A sprint isn't closed until its ledger, QA artifacts, and retro exist** — Sprint 10/11 show that skipping this doesn't block forward progress (Sprint 12 planning still happened), but it hides real state until something forces a reconciliation. Make retro/QA-plan/smoke-check part of the sprint's own Definition of Done, not a follow-up task (already true on paper per the sprint template — the gap was in practice, not the template).
2. **`/gate-check` is doing double duty as a ledger-integrity check.** It caught the 11-2/11-4/11-3/11-5 staleness this session purely as a side effect of validating the Production→Polish gate. Consider running it (or a lighter `/sprint-status`) more often than just at phase gates.

## Summary
The engineering held up — the era loop is real, tested, and (as of Sprint 12) finally playable end-to-end. The process didn't: two sprints closed with no QA sign-off, no retro, and a tracking ledger that silently diverged from reality for 12 days. Nothing was lost, but it was only caught because Sprint 12's gate-check happened to look. The fix is procedural, not technical — items 1-3 above are the whole story.
