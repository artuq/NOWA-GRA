## Store copy

### Short description

Build an influencer empire, survive the algorithm, and shape a satirical
career through choices that never forget.

### Description

King of Cringe is a satirical idle tycoon about climbing from an empty stream
to a full-blown content empire. Choose timed actions, grow your Reach, manage
Haters, Morale and Sponsors, then return later to collect clear offline
progress.

Your decisions become your career history. Swipe through internet disasters,
sponsor dilemmas and moral shortcuts, unlock distinct influencer paths, hire a
team, face Burnout, and start new eras with permanent bonuses. Optimize the
numbers—or embrace the cringe.

### Controls

- Mouse or touch: select actions and buttons; drag/swipe decision cards or use
  their visible left/right choices.
- Keyboard in skill challenges: `A` / Left Arrow moves left, `D` / Right Arrow
  moves right.
- The game is fully playable with mouse or touch; keyboard is optional.
